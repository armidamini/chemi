# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.
- Respect privacy and do not publish confidential or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional collaborative setting are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibility

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently exclude participants for behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
